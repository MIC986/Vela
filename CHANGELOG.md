# Vela 1.0.0

The first public release.

**Download `Vela_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` below.** It installs for your Windows account only, so no admin rights are
needed. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning: choose **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

## In this release

- **One library for every store:** Steam, Epic, the Xbox app and Game Pass, GOG Galaxy, EA, Ubisoft, Battle.net,
  Riot, Rockstar, Amazon Games and itch.io, plus games found in your own folders.
- **Home:** your recent games under their own artwork. Play, and Vela steps aside until you're done.
- **Library:** a full grid with filters and sorting, and a Browse view that sorts games into shelves, with a staged
  panel for each series it finds.
- **Statistics:** hours across every store, your most played games, genres, habits, library growth, ratings and
  storage.
- **Full screen:** a console-style mode with an intro, a quick menu and a choice of monitor.
- **Your own touches:** collections, renaming, your own covers and backgrounds, accent colors, background styles,
  interface size and sounds.
- **Every input:** works with a mouse, a keyboard or a controller, with an on-screen keyboard where typing is needed.
- **Privacy:** no accounts and no telemetry. Online artwork is opt-in and asked about on first launch.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
